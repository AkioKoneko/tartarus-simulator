How a canvas works

  1. The canvas. 6×6 cells: blank cells take paints, ice cells (blocked) and void cells don't. One Coloring Paint starts on the canvas, marked "!".
  2. The objective. The active Coloring Paint asks for paints of a type (monster, gameplay or any) in its range: its row, its column, its diagonals, the 4 cells next to it, or the whole canvas. Paints already there count; the Coloring Paint itself doesn't.
  3. Meeting it. Once enough are in range, every matching paint in range rises 1 or 2 levels, to Lv7 at most. The Treasure Chest is a gameplay paint, so gameplay and "any" objectives that reach it raise it.
  4. The next Coloring Paint. A new one is offered. Pick a brushstroke direction: Reimagine (change its target or range), Spread (open ice or void cells, or place it on a painted cell), or Layer (an extra effect when it's met). Then pick 1 of 2 effects, and place it.
  5. When stuck. Forced Color Mixing meets the objective right away, for Colored Snow (5, then 10, then 15; 3 a canvas).
  6. The end. The canvas is done when its cells are full. Then the painting becomes a map, and a Lv7 chest is where the money is.
