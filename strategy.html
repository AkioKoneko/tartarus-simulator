A day on the block

Every day runs in the same order. Here is what the strategy does at each step.

  1. Patrol

    Pick one of the three destinations. A locked door usually wins: it adds a new inmate, who may devour today from the warden's cell. Go to a known inmate instead when it has a strong devour waiting. A door with an event badge is a bonus, especially a Chain reaction. Prefer the farther destination when they're close, and don't open a rat nest before it hatches.

  2. Event

    Accept a Chain reaction. For an Execution, count the inmates it would remove against the tiers the executioner gains, and accept only if you come out ahead.

  3. Devour

    If the warden's inmate has a target, devour. Pick the first meal whose chain is worth the most: the Trainer shows each option's chain odds. After that bite the chain runs by itself and can't be stopped.

  4. Swap

    If the warden's inmate is low-tier, swap it. When it has nothing to eat, swap before the devour: the new inmate might have a target today.

  5. End the day

    Once nothing in the warden's cell can gain, end the day. The next morning, prey marks grow, nests hatch, and new destinations are drawn.

Chains: how many beasts you need

After every bite the eater rises a tier and re-rolls into a random beast. The chain continues if that new beast has something to eat at its new tier. With the single-meal chain each beast eats exactly one other: a Mammoth needs a Lion, a Lion needs a Wolf, a Wolf needs a Rat, and a Rat needs a Mammoth. So each beast waiting at a tier covers one of the four possible re-rolls.

The contract challenge

Twelve days, three contracts and 6 vouchers. You pass by delivering two contracts, one of them the heavy contract, which needs a Supreme or a vaulted Rainbow. Every inmate you send scores 2 to the power of its tier and adds about twice that in convoy pressure. So a light escort (1 voucher, pressure up to 36) carries at most 18 points, and a heavy escort (3 vouchers, up to 72) at most 36. A run tops out near 72 points, plus any bounty.

Convoy rules

  1. Never spend the heavy contract’s last voucher.The heavy contract needs at least one voucher, and the run can’t pass without it. Sending the most points on every contract can spend all six before day 12. That habit passed only of runs.
  2. Send the first two contracts by light escort.That keeps 3 vouchers for the heavy contract, enough for a heavy escort: the only way to ship a Rainbow (pressure 65), and the way to 36 points. Allowing heavy escorts on the first two passed , against .
  3. Send only what the contract needs.On the first two contracts, load the cheapest inmates that meet it, plus any spare Normals, and keep everything else for later.
  4. Get ready over the last before each delivery window.Check what the next contract needs and play for it: open doors when a Work gang or Mixed convoy needs more inmates, and hold on to Rares and Legendaries for the day-8 contract. Ignoring the next contract passed , against .
  5. Fill the heavy convoy.The heavy contract ends the run, so send the most points a heavy escort can carry: two Supremes and a Rare, or a Rainbow with a Magic and a Normal.

If live uses the two-meal chain

The Chinese sim uses a two-meal chain, where Mammoth also eats Wolf and Lion also eats Rat. The rules follow the season's video reveal. Odds and limits are practice values and may differ from the live game: new inmates roll 52% Normal, 27% Magic, 15% Rare and 6% Legendary, 18% of cells hide an event, and you get one devour and one swap a day over a 20-day drill.
